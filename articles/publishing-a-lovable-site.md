Publishing a [Lovable](https://lovable.dev/) site is quick:

1. While logged in, click **Preview** and confirm everything works.
2. Click **Publish**, then open the public subdomain in your browser.
3. Test the public URL in an incognito window so you know it works without being logged in to Lovable.
4. Add uptime monitoring so you know if the site goes down.

The easiest option I found is [HeyOnCall free website monitoring](https://heyoncall.com/free-website-monitoring), which is simple to set up and helps ensure Lovable doesn't take the site down without you noticing.

That's it, now you have a published Lovable site.

I did this for my resume site [noah-loves-code.lovable.app](https://noah-loves-code.lovable.app/)
